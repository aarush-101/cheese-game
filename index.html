A CRASH COURSE IN RAT ECONOMICS

CHEESE TALKS.
RATS LISTEN.

Get more of the 10 rats into your base than Crumb Bot before the 3-minute timer ends. A tie goes to sudden death: the first new capture wins.

Keep it cheesy. Stand in your base for 1 second to refill to 100. Each follower eats 2 cheese per second.

Home is where the cheese is. Free rats follow the best cheese-to-distance ratio within 12 units. Once they enter a healthy base, they stay and eat its unlimited cheese, ignoring both players. Only poison makes them leave.

Cause a stink. Carry cheese in your right hand and collected poison in your left. Aim near the center of a base and left click to throw poison. A hit disables its cheese for 15 seconds and scatters its rats. Pickups return after 20 seconds.

Take the high ground. Face a ladder and hold W to climb up or S to climb down. Space jumps, including off ladders and ledges. There is no fall damage. Rats and Crumb Bot use the ramps between floors—keep your herd close on the way up.
